# Misobus Internal Pages Topology

## Scope
Cloned internal routes:
- `/about`
- `/our-cars`
- `/cs`
- `/estimate`
- `/term-and-service`
- `/personal-info-policy`

Excluded by request:
- `/inquiry_estimate/*` private detail pages
- WordPress admin/login behavior
- author archives and test posts

## Shared Chrome
- Header: same logo/nav as homepage, active item underlined in cyan.
- Mobile: hamburger opens left drawer; body scroll locks while open.
- Footer: same footer links/social links/company line/copyright.
- Floating inquiry: fixed bottom-right, click-driven vertical menu.

## Page Models
- About: centered title CTA, full-width bus photo, dark process band, final brand statement.
- Our Cars: static carousel-like visual of bus + seat map, thumbnail row, “기타” section.
- Customer Support: gradient contact hero and FAQ cards.
- Estimate: static visual clone of public inquiry list. No backend submission and no detail-page cloning.
- Legal pages: breadcrumb plus text content block, shared footer/floating menu.
